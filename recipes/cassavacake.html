Cassava Cake

Description

Cassava cake is a classic, traditional Filipino baked dessert made from grated cassava root, coconut milk,
and condensed milk, usually finished with a rich, creamy custard layer on top

Ingredients

  • 2 cups grated, peeled cassava
  • 1 can coconut milk
  • 1 can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can evaporated milk
  • 2 large eggs;beaten

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C).
  2. Stir cassava, coconut milk, condensed milk, evaporated milk, and eggs together in a bowl until thoroughly combined
  3. Pour into a 2-quart baking dish
  4. Bake in the preheated oven until set, about 1 hour. Turn the broiler on and bake until top of cake is browned,
    2-3 mintues.Cool completely in the refrigerator before serving, about 1 hour
